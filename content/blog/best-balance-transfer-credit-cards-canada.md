## The Short Answer

The best balance transfer credit card in Canada is one that gives you enough time to repay the debt at a total cost you can afford. A 0% headline does not mean a free transfer: the transfer fee, annual fee and repayment deadline still matter.

Among the three offers reviewed here, **CIBC Select Visa has the lowest transfer fee**, **MBNA True Line Mastercard has no annual fee**, and **BMO Preferred Rate Mastercard offers the longest promotional runway**. Those are different advantages, not a universal winner.

Issuer terms checked **November 14, 2026**. This is a selected comparison, not an exhaustive ranking of every Canadian card. Offers, eligibility and provincial disclosures can change; confirm your application terms before transferring.

Amounts are rounded up to cents; adjust the final payment to the actual remaining balance. These are payoff targets, not contractual minimum payments. Always pay at least the statement minimum and clear the balance before the actual expiry date. A shortened first period, processing delay or late transfer can require a larger payment.

For example, someone with $5,000 to repay and $300 available per period could fit the 18-payment principal schedule, provided they can also cover its fees. The ten-payment schedule would require $500 per period. Choosing solely on the $50 transfer fee would leave that person with an unrealistic plan.

## How to Choose Between These Three Cards

### BMO Preferred Rate: More Time, With a Renewal Cost

BMO's promotional clock starts at account opening. Waiting to transfer does not restart it. The offer excludes transfers from BMO accounts, and the transferred amount plus its fee must fit the available credit limit. The promotion does not make purchases or fees interest-free.

A useful budgeting question is whether you really need all 18 periods. If you can repay before renewal, your costs may differ from our full-window example. Confirm fee timing and any account changes directly with BMO.

### MBNA True Line: A No-Annual-Fee Repayment Option

Qualifying MBNA transfers must happen within 90 days of opening. Balances from MBNA or other TD Bank Group accounts are excluded. Missing a required minimum payment can end the promotional treatment under the offer terms.

For a $5,000 balance, our twelve-payment plan requires about $416.67 per period, plus the separate $150 fee. Work that amount into your budget before treating a year at 0% as sufficient time.

### CIBC Select Visa: Lower Fee, Shorter Runway

CIBC requires the promotional transfer request with the online application and limits it to 50% of the assigned credit limit. That means transferring $5,000 would require at least a $10,000 assigned limit, subject to the issuer's approval and terms.

CIBC also warns that carrying the promotional balance affects the interest-free treatment of new purchases. Keeping spending off the transfer card makes the repayment plan easier to follow.

## Build a Repayment Plan Before Applying

1. **Write down the balance to move.** Include any amount that must remain on the old account because of transfer limits.
2. **Calculate the transfer fee.** Multiply the amount by the fee percentage and check for a minimum fee.
3. **Use the actual expiry date.** Divide principal by the number of payments you can make before it, rather than assuming the headline month count applies exactly.
4. **Budget fees separately.** Ask how payments are allocated and whether the transfer fee attracts interest.
5. **Keep paying the original account until the transfer posts.** Check both accounts instead of assuming the request has completed.
6. **Review each statement.** A payoff plan does not replace the issuer's minimum-payment requirement.

If the required payment is unaffordable, compare a sustainable lower-rate repayment option before taking on another account. Our [guide to credit card interest rates](/blog/credit-card-interest-rates-canada) explains how carrying a balance changes the decision.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is a 0% Balance Transfer Free?

Not necessarily. In these examples, the transfer fee is between $30 and $300, depending on the amount and offer, before any renewal fee or interest on fees. Compare total dollars paid, not just the promotional APR.

### What Happens When the Promotion Ends?

Any remaining transferred balance becomes subject to the applicable standard rate in your agreement. Our table shows the published standard transfer rates checked for this article. Your statement and disclosure determine the rate and exact date that apply to you.

### Can I Transfer a Balance From Any Bank?

Do not assume so. Same-bank and related-bank exclusions, transfer limits and application rules vary. Verify the sending account is eligible before applying for the new card.

### Should I Use a Rewards Card Instead?

For a balance you cannot immediately repay, borrowing cost should come first. Rewards on future spending do not reduce the interest rate on existing debt. Once the debt is cleared and you can pay purchases in full, use the [ClearFin rewards calculator](/credit-card-calculator-canada) to compare everyday spending value.
